OLA Ride Analytics Dashboard

The Business Question: Where are ride bookings, revenue, and service quality being lost across customer and driver cancellations?

The Dataset: Analyzed 100,000+ Ola ride booking records covering vehicle types, cancellation reasons, ride distances, and payment channels.

100K+ Booking Records 5 Interactive Pages 10 Complex Queries ₹34M+ Revenue 28.08% Cancellation
Recommendation Breakdown
1. Finding Driver cancellations spike during peak evening commute hours due to distance/traffic.
2. Why It Matters Driver cancellations cause lost customer trust, lower completion rates, and revenue leakage.
3. Action Implement prime-time driver surge bonus multipliers and distance disclosure thresholds.
SQL CTE & Cancellation Analysis Query MySQL 8.0 
WITH CancellationSummary AS (
        SELECT 
        Booking_ID,
        Vehicle_Type,
        Booking_Status,
        Canceled_Rides_by_Driver,
        Booking_Value
    FROM ola_bookings
)
SELECT 
    Vehicle_Type,
    COUNT(Booking_ID) AS Total_Rides,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Booking_Status = 'Success' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS Completed_Rides,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Canceled_Rides_by_Driver = 'Personal & Car related issue' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS Driver_Personal_Cancels,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN Booking_Status = 'Canceled by Driver' THEN Booking_Value ELSE 0 END), 2) AS Lost_Revenue_INR
FROM CancellationSummary
GROUP BY Vehicle_Type
ORDER BY Lost_Revenue_INR DESC;
What it does & Why it matters: Uses a Common Table Expression (CTE) and conditional aggregations to calculate exact revenue loss from driver cancellations per vehicle tier, helping fleet operations pinpoint where to deploy driver incentives.

Repeat_Customer_Revenue_PCT = 
VAR TotalRevenue = SUM(CustomerShopping[Purchase Amount (USD)])
VAR RepeatCustomerRevenue = 
    CALCULATE(
        SUM(CustomerShopping[Purchase Amount (USD)]),
        CustomerShopping[Previous Purchases] > 5
    )
RETURN 
    DIVIDE(RepeatCustomerRevenue, TotalRevenue, 0)
What it does & Why it matters: Calculates the percentage of total sales generated by loyal repeat buyers (>5 past orders) to validate marketing retention spend efficiency.
