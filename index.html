I agree. 👍 For a **Data Analyst portfolio**, you don't need the workflow section if space is limited. These **4 additions** give you the biggest return: ## 🥇 1. Business Case Study (Must Have) Each project gets its own page with: * Business Problem * Dataset * Objectives * Analysis * Key Insights * Business Recommendations * Conclusion This is what separates a project from a dashboard. --- ## 🥈 2. Live Dashboard (Must Have) Embed your Power BI dashboard so recruiters can interact with it. Seeing a live dashboard is far more convincing than screenshots. --- ## 🥉 3. SQL Analysis Section For each project, include: * Business Question * SQL Query * Output * Why that query mattered It proves you can answer business questions with SQL. --- ## 🏅 4. Business Insights & Recommendations (Very Important) This is where most fresher portfolios are weak. Don't stop at charts. End every project with: **Key Insights** * What patterns did you discover? **Business Recommendations** * What actions should the business take? **Expected Impact** * How could those actions improve revenue, retention, cost, or operations? --- ### If I were a hiring manager... A portfolio with these four sections would impress me **far more** than one with animations, visitor counters, AI chatbots, or fancy UI effects. Your portfolio should communicate one message: > **"I don't just build dashboards—I solve business problems using data."** That's the impression that gets interviews.
